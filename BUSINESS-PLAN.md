# $0 → $25,000 Plan — Web Services Business (Refined)

**Starting point:** new to freelancing, full-time hours, no portfolio, M-Pesa + PayPal/Wise ready, stack = Next.js + Tailwind.
**Targets (first):** Kenyan SMEs → diaspora clients → international (Upwork/Fiverr/LinkedIn Services).
**Niche:** restaurants & cafes in Nairobi (backup: cleaning/home services).
**Revenue model:** productized services first; digital products later.

> Targets, not guarantees. Results depend on outreach volume, work quality, consistency. Not financial advice.

## Offer ladder (USD)
| Offer | Price | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Landing page / business site | $150–$300 launch, $500–$800 standard | Entry offer, fast cash |
| Full site + SEO + copy (package) | $1,500–$3,000 | Main revenue driver (later) |
| Monthly retainer (hosting, updates, SEO report) | $300–$1,000/mo — push from client #1 | Recurring income |
| Templates / starter kits (Phase 4) | $29–$99 | Passive income |

## Math to $10K/month (steady state)
- 6–8 retainers × $400 = $2,400–$3,200
- 3 full-site projects × $2,000 = $6,000
- Small projects/templates × $500–$1,000
- **≈ $9–10K/month**

## Phase 0 (Weeks 1–2): Build proof before outreach
1. Niche locked: restaurants & cafes, Nairobi.
2. Build 2–3 demo/spec sites (fictional or public-info businesses, labeled as demos).
3. Build own sales site (Next.js + Tailwind) — offer, demos, pricing, WhatsApp button, contact form.
4. Set up: Google Business Profile, Upwork, Fiverr, LinkedIn Services, PayPal/Wise/M-Pesa.
5. One-page client agreement template with 50% upfront.

## Phase 1 (Weeks 3–6): First cash
- 10–15 targeted outreach/day (70–100/week), lead with demo site link.
- 2–3 discounted launch clients ($150–$300) in exchange for testimonials + reviews + referrals.
- Every project includes retainer pitch.
- **Target:** $500–$1,500 collected, 2–3 real case studies.

## Phase 2 (Weeks 7–12): Systemize + recurring
- Reusable restaurant-site template system; each build in days not weeks.
- Standard pricing $500–$800 entry.
- Daily LinkedIn/X post + 2 short before/after videos/week (IG/TikTok).
- Upwork/Fiverr push with demo portfolio; raise prices after every 2–3 clients.
- Ask every client for 2 referrals.
- **Target:** $2,000–$4,000/month.

## Phase 3 (Months 4–6): Scale
- Raise prices again; subcontract junior dev/designer for overflow.
- Upsells: branding, e-commerce, M-Pesa integration, SEO, email marketing, social management.
- Paid ads ($5–$10/day Meta/Google) only once an offer is proven to convert.
- **Target:** $4,000–$6,000/month.

## Phase 4 (Months 7–12): Build the asset
- Stack retainers to $4–6K MRR; launch template pack / "website in a weekend" course from own site.
- Validate niche product (restaurant website builder / productized platform).
- **Target:** $8,000–$10,000+/month; ~$25K cumulative around months 7–10.

## Weekly rhythm
- Mon–Tue: outreach (50+ messages) · Wed–Fri: client delivery · Daily: 1 content piece · Fri: numbers review (leads, calls, closes, revenue, MRR).

## Funnel targets
Outreach → reply 10–15% · reply → call 30–40% · call → close 25–35%. ~30–50 messages per 1 client per week (more early on).

## Risks
Underpricing · niche drift · no contract/50% upfront · payment delays · international platforms slow to warm up — local outreach pays first.
