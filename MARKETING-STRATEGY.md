# Nairobi Stack Studio — Marketing Strategy
**Positioning:** Nairobi Stack Studio is a tech company (not a freelancer) that builds websites, web apps/MVPs, e-commerce stores and growth/SEO programs for Kenyan startups & SMEs, diaspora-owned businesses and international startups.
**One-liner:** "We build the web presence — and the product — your business runs on."
**Why this works:** "Freelancer" competes on price; "tech company / studio" competes on trust, process and retainer value. Same skills, 2–5× deal sizes.

## Ideal customer profiles (ICPs)
1. **Kenyan startups (pre-seed → Series A)** — need MVP/web app fast, $2K–$15K builds. Reach: iHub/Nailab/Strathmore communities, LinkedIn, demo days.
2. **Kenyan SMEs & established businesses** — restaurants are one vertical, but now generalised: clinics, schools, law firms, logistics. $500–$3,000 sites + retainers.
3. **Diaspora-owned businesses** — US/UK-based Kenyans needing sites/e-commerce for businesses back home or abroad. Trust via diaspora networks, WhatsApp groups, M-Pesa + PayPal.
4. **International startups (Upwork/remote)** — $30–$80/hr or fixed-price MVP sprints, USD 3–10× local rates. Lower volume, higher margin, builds reviews.

## Offer ladder
| Offer | Price | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Website Sprint (1–2 weeks) | $500–$1,500 | Entry, fast cash, low risk for client |
| Full business package (site + SEO + copy) | $1,500–$3,000 | Core revenue |
| MVP / web app sprint (Next.js/Supabase) | $3,000–$15,000 | High-ticket anchor |
| E-commerce (Shopify/Next + M-Pesa) | $2,000–$8,000 | High-ticket |
| Growth retainer (SEO, content, updates, hosting) | $300–$1,000/mo | MRR engine — attach to every deal |

## Channel plan (90 days)
**Days 1–30 — Foundation + proof**
- Rebrand all assets: sales site, Google Business Profile, LinkedIn/X/IG, Upwork/Fiverr profiles. One consistent logo/color system.
- 2–3 portfolio pieces in NEW positioning (a startup MVP demo, an SME redesign before/after, an e-commerce demo with M-Pesa).
- Claim/optimize Google Business Profile; ask 3 early clients for reviews.
- Start founder-led content on LinkedIn + X: 1 post/day (builds, lessons, before/afters, pricing teardowns).

**Days 31–60 — Outbound + inbound engines**
- Cold outreach 15–20/day (WhatsApp/email/LinkedIn) to 100 targeted Kenyan startups & SMEs — lead with free 3-point audit + relevant demo link.
- Upwork: 10 proposals/day, fixed-price "Website Sprint" and "MVP Sprint" listings.
- SEO content: 2 articles/week targeting "web design Nairobi", "website developer Kenya", "MVP development Kenya", "e-commerce website Kenya M-Pesa".
- Referral program: 10% of contract value (or 1 free month retainer) per referred client who signs.

**Days 61–90 — Scale + authority**
- Partnerships: 3 local accelerators/tech hubs (iHub, Nailab, Strathmore @iLab), 2 digital agencies (white-label dev), 2 lawyers/accountants who serve SMEs.
- Webinars/workshops: "Website in a weekend" free session → warm leads.
- Case study videos: 1 client story/week.
- Paid: only after an offer converts — $5–10/day Meta (local SMEs) and Google (high-intent keywords).
- Diaspora push: 5 diaspora WhatsApp/Facebook groups, 1 diaspora-focused landing page with PayPal/Zelle instructions.

## Content pillars (founder-led, LinkedIn/X/IG)
1. Builds & demos (before/after, screen recordings) — 40%
2. Lessons from client work (pricing, scope, tools) — 25%
3. Local tech ecosystem insights (startups, M-Pesa, internet trends) — 20%
4. Offer-led posts (sprint slots, retainer value) — 15%

## Funnel & metrics
Outreach → reply (10–15%) → audit/call (30–40%) → quote (70%+) → close (25–35%).
Track weekly: leads by channel, CAC, close rate, avg deal size, retainer attach rate (target ≥60%), MRR, churn.
**North-star:** $10K/mo = ~3 deals/mo ($2K avg) + $2–3K MRR retainers + 1 MVP deal ($4K+) quarterly.

## Budget (lean, $0–300/mo)
- $0: organic social, SEO, referrals, partnerships, Upwork (free tier).
- ~$50–150: domain + hosting + email (Google Workspace optional), stock photos.
- $50–100/mo (from month 2+): boosted posts / small Google Ads test ONLY after one offer converts.
- Time > money: 40+ hrs/week founder time is the real budget; outsource design/junior dev only after paid work exceeds capacity.

## Positioning vs competitors
- vs. "cheap freelancer": you sell process, contract, fixed price, support.
- vs. agencies: you're faster, leaner, startup-priced.
- vs. foreign remote devs: local context, M-Pesa/KRA/payments fluency, same timezone — huge for SMEs.

## 12-month arc
Mon 1–2: proof + rebrand + first Sprint clients. Mon 3–4: retainers + SEO flywheel + Upwork reviews. Mon 5–6: first MVP/e-comm deal; raise Website Sprint price. Mon 7–9: subcontract junior dev; $6–8K/mo. Mon 10–12: productize (template pack / "launch kit for Kenyan startups"); $8–12K/mo run rate.
