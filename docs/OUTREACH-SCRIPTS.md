# Outreach Scripts — Restaurants & Cafes, Nairobi

Rule: lead with the demo, not yourself. Keep under 60 words. One ask. Personalize line 1.

## WhatsApp / Instagram DM (cold, first message)
Hi [Name], I found [Business] on Google/Instagram — love your [specific dish/vibe]. I build websites for Nairobi restaurants and made a quick demo of what yours could look like: [demo link]. No strings — if you like it I can set it up from $150 (menu, WhatsApp ordering, Google profile). Want the link details or a free 3-point audit of your current online presence?

## Follow-up (2 days later, no reply)
Hi [Name], just bumping this — here's the demo again: [link]. Happy to send you a free mini-audit (3 quick wins to get more customers from Google/IG). Want it?

## Follow-up 2 (5 days later, last)
Hi [Name] — last message, promise. If a website isn't a priority now, no problem at all. If you ever want one, we start at $150 and it takes under a week. Have a great service!

## After they reply "tell me more"
Awesome. Quick context: I do fixed-price sites for restaurants — menu, WhatsApp click-to-order, Google Business Profile, and a monthly option for updates/SEO from $300/mo. Want a free 10-min audit call or should I send a quote straight away? If quote: just need your menu (photo/PDF is fine), logo if you have one, and 3 photos.

## LinkedIn (owners / managers)
Hi [Name], I help Nairobi restaurants get more walk-ins and orders with fast, mobile-first websites (WhatsApp ordering + M-Pesa checkout). I built a demo for a restaurant like yours: [link]. Would a quick free audit of your online presence be useful? 3 quick wins, no obligation.

## Upwork / Fiverr proposal (international)
Hi [Client], I saw your post about [their need]. I build Next.js + Tailwind websites for restaurants/small businesses — fast, mobile-first, SEO-ready. Demo of similar work: [link]. Fixed price $X, delivered in Y days, includes 1 month of support. Happy to start with a small milestone. What does your menu/content look like?

## Google Business Profile / Maps lead-in
[Find businesses with no website / broken site / no Google profile]
Hi [Name], I came across [Business] on Google Maps and noticed you don't have a website yet — most of your competitors in [area] do. I can set up a simple, fast site with your menu + WhatsApp ordering from $150 and get your Google profile optimized so you show up in local searches. Want a free mockup of your homepage?

## Closing / getting to yes
Great — to lock your slot this week: 50% to start ([M-Pesa till / PayPal]), draft in 3 days, live within a week. I'll also throw in a Google Business Profile setup free. And if you're happy, a short testimonial + Google review would mean a lot. Shall I send the one-page agreement?

## Referral ask (after delivery)
Glad you're happy with the site! Quick favor: do you know 2 other restaurant/cafe owners who need a stronger online presence? I'd appreciate the intro — and I'll give you [10% off retainer / a free update] for each one who signs.

## Retainer pitch (end of every project)
Now it's live, want me to keep it fresh? $300/mo covers hosting, domain, menu updates, and a monthly Google/SEO report so you keep showing up. Most restaurants see it pay for itself in 1–2 extra orders a month. Want to add it?

## Tracking (per week)
Outreach sent | replies | audits booked | quotes sent | closes | revenue | MRR added. Aim: 70–100 outreach/week, 10–15% reply, 30–40% reply→call, 25–35% call→close.
